# All apps

Updated 2026-10-25 11:38, at 1.0.1.

Total screen time for a day or a week, when in it you used the phone, and
which apps took it.

## Sub-features

- **Daily or Weekly**, and arrows to step back and forward through periods,
  from the first logged event to now.
- **Total screen time** for the period.
- **Usage chart**: by hour for a day, by weekday for a week.
- **Most used**: apps by time, each with a bar against the top app. Tap one
  for [One app](one-app.md).

## How to get to it

Open Timekeep and swipe left from the Activity log.
