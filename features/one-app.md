# One app

Updated 2026-10-23 00:01, at 1.0.1.

One app's time for a day or a week, compared with the period before, and its
sessions.

## Sub-features

- **Daily or Weekly**, and arrows to step through periods.
- **Daily average** for the period, and the change from the period before as
  a percentage.
- The app's **sessions** in the period, with start, end and length.

## How to get to it

Tap a session in the Activity log, or an app under Most used in All apps.

## Driving it

No check yet. No test target.

By hand only.

## Gotchas

- Apps are told apart by the name Shortcuts reports, so a renamed app starts
  a new history.
